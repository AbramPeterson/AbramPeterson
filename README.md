* **🖥️ Infrastructure & Networking:** Designing high-availability networks, managing **Proxmox** clusters, **pfSense** firewalls, **Docker** containers, and telemetry with **Zabbix**.
* **🎓 Education & Credentials:** M.S. & B.S. in Cybersecurity | **CompTIA CASP+**, **PenTest+**, **CySA+**, **Security+**, and **ISC2 CC**.
